Academic profile

Anatomy, education & clinical relevance

Myroslava Kumka, MD, PhD, is an anatomy professor and researcher. Her work explores the structures and variations of the human body, with particular attention to their relevance in clinical practice.

Her research interests include musculoskeletal and neurovascular variation, the classification of fascia, and the innervation of vertebral arteries.

She joined Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College (CMCC) in the fall of 2000. Her roles there included Associate Professor and Anatomy Department Chair; she served as Chair from 2008 to 2020. Her teaching encompassed clinical gross anatomy, neuroanatomy, embryology, histology and histopathology, as well as continuing education.
